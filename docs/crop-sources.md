# Crop sources for `crops.csv`

Starting material for the engine owner (package 02) to fill `data/catalog/crops.csv`. Every fact below was checked in the source on 2026-11-14; page or table references are given so you can cite them. **The tier values (low/medium/high) are still yours to decide**, with the source written in the `source` column. Do not copy a number from here without opening the source.

## Per crop

| `CropId` | Verified facts (source) | Suggested `local_source` |
|---|---|---|
| `maiz` | Lowland varieties: ICTA B-5 "can be bent at 80 days and harvested at 95", recommended for low or badly distributed rain, fits intercrop with bean and sorghum; ICTA A-6 cycle 90 days, can go in intercrop or relay with sesame or sorghum; hybrids HB-83 / HB-PROTICTA harvested at 120 days (ICTA-MAIZ, pp. 22-23). Jutiapa, Chiquimula, Jalapa, Zacapa and El Progreso are the "limited moisture" zone with recurrent drought (ICTA-MAIZ). Root depth 1.0-1.7 m, p = 0.55 (FAO-56 "Maize, Field (grain)"). ECOCROP: https://ecocrop.apps.fao.org/ecocrop/srv/en/dataSheet?id=2175 | true |
| `frijol` | ICTA Chortí 75 days, ICTA Patriarca 80, ICTA Ligero 65, ICTA Tahual 80 days to harvest (CGIAR-FRIJOL, variety table). In the east, sow between 15 August and 15 September; three systems: relay into maize, intercrop with maize, monocrop (CGIAR-FRIJOL, "Siembra"). Root depth 0.6-0.9 m, p = 0.45 (FAO-56 "Beans, dry and Pulses"). Fixes nitrogen. ECOCROP: https://ecocrop.apps.fao.org/ecocrop/srv/en/dataSheet?id=1668 | true |
| `maiz_frijol` | Intercrop: both sown at the same place and time (CGIAR-FRIJOL, "asocio"). Combine the `maiz` and `frijol` sources; document how you derived each tier. | true |
| `maicillo` | ICTA Rendidor: photo-insensitive white grain sorghum for "segunda" (ICTA-SORGO 2019); traditional crop in southeastern Jutiapa (USAC-JUTIAPA); harvest 105-110 days per ICTA as reported by AGN (verify in an ICTA sheet before citing). Root depth 1.0-2.0 m, p = 0.55 (FAO-56 "Sorghum, grain"). ECOCROP: https://ecocrop.apps.fao.org/ecocrop/srv/en/dataSheet?id=48747 | true if you cite ICTA |
| `gandul` | *Cajanus cajan*. ICTA research in Guatemala's Dry Corridor: "mucuna, gandul y frijol cachito son especies que se adaptan a esa región"; the grain of gandul is eaten (fresh with rice, meat or tamales; dry like beans) and it is also forage; it produces pods all year (ICTA-ABONOS pp. 2-4). Legume, fixes nitrogen. **Cycle:** ECOCROP gives 90-365 days, life span perennial (https://ecocrop.apps.fao.org/ecocrop/srv/en/dataSheet?id=576). Secondary source (EcuRed, https://www.ecured.cu/Cajanus_Cajan): early varieties 90-150 days, semi-late 150-220, late > 220; semi-perennial types flower once a year (November-January). Fits as a primera crop with `none` in postrera. Suggested `cycle_days` 180 (a semi-late type sown in May, harvested around November); mark it as an estimate. Root depth: not in FAO-56 Table 22. | true for facts taken from ICTA-ABONOS |
| `mucuna` | *Mucuna pruriens*. Adapts to the Dry Corridor; among mucuna and frijol cachito it showed the highest nitrogen fixation, and ICTA still promotes it in maize systems (ICTA-ABONOS p. 4). Green manure, not a food crop: income tier should reflect that. **Cycle:** "Life cycles range from 100 to 300 d to harvest of the pod"; in northern Honduras all fields "begin flowering in early to mid-October, regardless of the planting date"; farmers slash it once mature, "some 8 months" after it is introduced between the maize rows (Buckles, Triomphe & Sain 1998, IDRC/CIMMYT, https://idrc-crdi.ca/sites/default/files/openebooks/269-4/index.html). ECOCROP has no cycle values for it (https://ecocrop.apps.fao.org/ecocrop/srv/en/dataSheet?id=2350). **Modelling choice for the engine owner:** flowering is fixed by day length, so the cycle depends on the sowing date. As a postrera crop sown in September it flowers in October and covers the field into the dry season; `cycle_days` 150 (September to January) is a reasonable estimate, marked as such. Its point is soil cover and nitrogen, so `market_tier` low. | true for facts taken from ICTA-ABONOS |
| `canavalia` | ICTA lists it among the green manures with the best results for maize systems; some farmers intercrop maize with canavalia (two seeds every three maize hills, sown together or up to 15 days after maize) (ICTA-ABONOS pp. 2-4). ECOCROP: https://ecocrop.apps.fao.org/ecocrop/srv/en/dataSheet?id=609. Not in FAO-56 Table 22. Fixes nitrogen. | true for facts taken from ICTA-ABONOS |
| `ajonjoli` | ECOCROP: cycle about 40-180 days. https://ecocrop.apps.fao.org/ecocrop/srv/en/dataSheet?id=1937. Root depth 1.0-1.5 m, p = 0.60 (FAO-56 "Sesame"). ICTA-MAIZ mentions sesame in relay or intercrop with maize. Grown mainly in Retalhuleu and Suchitepéquez (MAGA-AJONJOLI); traditional crop in southeastern Jutiapa (USAC-JUTIAPA). | false for ECOCROP/FAO values |
| `ayote` | *Cucurbita moschata*. ECOCROP: fruits harvested 80-140 days after sowing. https://ecocrop.apps.fao.org/ecocrop/srv/en/dataSheet?id=820. Root depth 1.0-1.5 m, p = 0.35 (FAO-56 "Pumpkin, Winter Squash"). Grown with maize and bean in the milpa of the Dry Corridor of Jalapa (USAC-AYOTE). | false for ECOCROP/FAO values |

## Notes

- **Calendar check:** `calendar.py` pins primera in May and postrera in September. CGIAR-FRIJOL gives 15 August - 15 September for postrera bean in the east, so September is consistent. With maize 120 days (May-Aug) and bean 75 days (Sep-Nov), the bare months are Dec-Apr = 5, matching the spec test.
- **p (depletion fraction):** a lower p means the crop suffers sooner when the soil dries. It can back `drought_tolerance` or `water_need`, but the tier cut-offs are your call: write them in `crops.csv`'s `source` text or in a comment at the top of the test.
- **Still missing:** Guatemalan attribute values for ajonjolí and ayote, and a current ICTA sheet for maicillo days to harvest. ICTA's regional centre in Zacapa or MAGA extension material are the likely places.
